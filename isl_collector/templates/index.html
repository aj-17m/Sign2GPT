Help Build an ISL → English Translator

Upload a short Indian Sign Language video and type what it means in English.

How it works

  1. Record yourself signing one sentence (3–10 seconds)
  2. Upload the video below
  3. Type the English meaning
  4. Submit — your data is saved instantly

Tips for good recordings

  • Plain background (white or gray wall)
  • Good lighting, facing you
  • Camera at chest height, upper body visible
  • Solid color clothes (no patterns)
  • Keep it 3–10 seconds, one sentence per video
