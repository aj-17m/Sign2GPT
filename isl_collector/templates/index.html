📋 Tips for good recordings

  • Plain background — white/gray wall works best
  • Good lighting — face well-lit from the front
  • Camera at chest height — show your whole upper body
  • Sign cleanly — pause briefly before and after
  • One sentence per video — keep it 3-10 seconds
  • Solid color clothes — no busy patterns

🎯 What we need

We're collecting ~1000 short ISL clips paired with English translations to train an open-source sign language translator. Every submission helps make sign language tech accessible.

Sample sentences to try:

  • "hello how are you" / "good morning" / "thank you"
  • "my name is [name]"
  • "i am hungry" / "i need water"
  • "today is monday" / "it is raining"
  • "i live in [city]"
